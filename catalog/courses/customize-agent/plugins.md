## What this lesson covers

- A plugin bundles skills, hooks, MCP, agents, and commands
- Attach for one conversation or auto-load for a team
- Review trust before you enable auto-load
- Official examples live in the extensions repo

## Outline

1. Plugin vs single skill
2. Browse and attach in Canvas
3. Conversation-only vs marketplace auto-load
4. When a company should govern the catalog

## Try this

Open Plugins in Canvas, inspect one official plugin, and attach it to a throwaway conversation. Do not auto-load it yet.
