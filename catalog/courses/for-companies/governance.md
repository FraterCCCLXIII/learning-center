## What this lesson covers

- Scope: which secrets, tools, and domains an agent may reach
- Approval and halt on risky actions
- RBAC, SSO, audit logs
- Cost and budget windows for finance — without giving them the IDE

## Outline

1. Secrets in settings, never in prompts
2. Confirmation policy for destructive tools
3. Org roles and conversation sharing
4. Budgets and spend alerts

List secrets an automation would need. Put them in backend secrets. Confirm a teammate without that role cannot read the values.
